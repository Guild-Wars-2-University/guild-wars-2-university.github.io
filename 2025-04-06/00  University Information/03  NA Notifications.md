# Content Roles Self-Assignment
### Each of these buttons will give you specific roles that allow you to access a different part of the discord for that content. It will also give you notifications whenever people are looking to form groups for that content. 
Simply click the buttons bellow each message to sign-up for that content. You can click them again to remove yourself from that content if you wish.

---

## Open World
```yaml
1-50 player open content
Content for all levels
``` 
Open World encompasses everything open world and story related content.  
- <@&838153172085637210> : Social events and trains
  - Guild Missions, fishing parties, etc.
  - Achievement Farming - Going for some specific achievements in story instances/open world.
  - Map/World Completion - 100% completion on a specific map. Completing every heart, viewing every vista, finding every point of interest, earning every hero challenge.
  - Hero Point Trains - Farm the expansion maps for all the hero challenges to help unlock your elite specializations. 
  - Bounty Train - Farm the Path of Fire expansion maps for bounties. 
- <@&1319647922991661056> : (level 80) Group up in some expansion maps with many players to tackle big event chains called meta events. 
See https://discord.com/channels/446550730640195586/1138843634729500753 for more details.

## Fractals
```yaml
5 player instance content
Recommended for level 71+
```
Fractals are great for learning how to play instanced group content and earning high quality gear.
- <@&772184602227245056> : essentially dungeons. No agony resistance (AR) required. 
- <@&831639754495623178> : similar to T1 but harder. 50+ AR required. 
- <@&831639766293413908> : much harder version of T2. 100+ AR required.
- <@&831638783765905409> : very hard version of T3. 150+ AR required. 
- <@&831639946451222589> : You have completed fractals on normal and are looking for training, progression, or clears of the CMs. Please only sign up for content you meet the requirements of.
See https://discord.com/channels/446550730640195586/771110507758354442 for more details.

## World Versus World
```yaml
1-50 player vs player content
Recommended for level 80
```
WvW is very different from PvE. You need to be experienced in fighting against other players while also good at working together in a group. WvW is also the most graphically intense gamemode, so you may need to lower your graphic settings.
- <@&772184719286599781>: lessons on how to survive group fights in WvW. 
- <@&925531339841761300> : Notifications for WvW groups running (Must either be in the GWU guild and have it selected as WvW team, or be in another guild from the same team).
See https://discord.com/channels/446550730640195586/771894823074398209 for more details.
