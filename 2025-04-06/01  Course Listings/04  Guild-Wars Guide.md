# **What is Guild Wars**

Guild Wars 2 (Gw2) is a Massive Multiplayer Online Role Playing Game (MMORPG) where you can play five different races and learn the fighting styles of nine professions, in three different main game modes: Player-versus-Environment (PvE), Player-versus-Player (PvP), and World-versus-World (WvW). The largest gamemode in Gw2 is player-versus-environment (PvE). Gw2 boasts one of the biggest open world maps, with each expansion expanding the map further. Each map contains several dynamic events which will change the map depending on if the event is a success or a failure, giving you a truly living world to explore. You can also team up with your friends and join instanced content, which can be anything from a five-person dungeon delve to a fifty-person boss fight. With 92 different instances of varying types, there is something for everyone. When it comes to player-versus-player (PvP) combat, few MMOs can ever aspire to do it better than Gw2. For those wanting a more traditional experience, structured PvP lets you fight in team deathmatch and objective-based modes that focus on skill and coordination. And then there's the world-versus-world (WvW) mode, where six different servers come together to wage war across sprawling maps with hundreds of players at once. Participating in sieges and large-scale battles is the kind of fantastical fulfillment you dreamed of as a kid.

The heart of what makes Gw2 fun to play is all in its action combat, which emphasizes dodging and movement instead of memorizing complex skill rotations. You'll weave in and out of range of your opponents while unleashing flashy abilities—of which there are many to choose from. Each class is quite flexible in how you want to play, giving theorycrafters enough material to chisel away at their perfect build.

## **Guild Wars 2 Most Notable Features:**

- Massive open world full of dynamic and rewarding events.
- Action-tab hybrid combat with minimal animation locks.
- No kill stealing! Everyone gets XP and loot for events.
- Repeatable endgame PvE with dungeons, fractals, strikes and raids.
- Standardized PvP to try out builds on an even playing field with your peers from level 2.
- No gear grinding! Level cap of 80 does not increase and your endgame gear will stay relevant forever.
- Large scale open world PvP (called World-versus-World or WvW)!
- One of the most supportive, friendly, and helpful communities you'll find in any mmo game.

# **How to Install**

First you need to create an account with the developer of Guild Wars 2, ArenaNet (ANet). Go to [https://account.arena.net/register](https://account.arena.net/register "https://account.arena.net/register") in order to create an account. Your account name will be visible to everyone and the main way that people identify you in the game (often what people call you). It is not recommend that you use your real name as this could lead to stalking and cyber bullying. Once you create an account, log-in and navigate to "Game Content". Here you will be able to install the game client for free. Once you downloaded the client, open the application and select an install folder where you want to store the 50 GB of files for the game. The client will then begin to install the game. This could take several hours depending on your internet speed. The game will install all essential files first. This will allow you to begin playing the game before the download is complete. However, you may find your game-play interrupted to install a new map you're attempting to travel to if you choose to pause the download.

If you wish to purchase the expansion for the game you can do so either through the gemstore in-game, or here: [https://buy.guildwars2.com/](https://buy.guildwars2.com/ "https://buy.guildwars2.com/") Once you purchase the game you will be sent an email with your access key to unlock the game. Navigate back to your ANet account and select "Redeem a Code", then enter your access key to unlock the content on your account (if you purchase through the gemstore the key will automatically be added to your account). **Save your access key as this will be how ANet verifies your identity if you find yourself locked out of your account!**

When you first log into the game, you will be prompted to pick a server. There are two regions for the servers: NA and EU. **If you are on an NA server, you cannot play with people on EU servers and vise versa.** Otherwise, for PvE and PvP, the regional servers are all combined. The only place where the servers are separated is for WvW. It is recommend that you select a server that has a very high population, as this will give you more people to play with. Our WvW group is on Borlis Pass [NA] and Fissure of Woe [EU]. You can transfer servers later if you want, but it will cost money.

# **Character Creation**

When you make a character in the game you are fist asked what race you want to play: Human, Norn, Charr, Sylvari, and Asura. These are for aesthetic and story and won't have any real effect on your game-play. Afterwards you will be asked to choose a class. This is what really matters for your character. There are 9 core classes to the game (8 if you do not have the expansion). If you have the expansion, each class also has three elite specializations that you can unlock which allow for more diverse builds. The classes are separated into 3 groups based what type of armor they use:

### **+ Heavy Armor +**

- **Guardian** - Guardians are front-line support, they can grant boons to allies while simultaneously smacking down enemies.
  - Dragonhunter - Power Damage
  - Firebrand - Condition Damage or Boon Support and Heals
  - Willbender - Condition or Power Damage
- **Warrior** - Warriors are tanks, they can sustain a lot of damage while simultaneously dealing a lot in return.
  - Berserker - Power Damage with lots of CC
  - Spellbreaker - Power Damage (strategically counter enemies attacks to use their own power against them)
  - Bladesworn - Power Damage (sacrificing toughness for damage output)
- **Revenant (only available with the expansion)** - Full blown support class with lots of utility
  - Herald - Boon Support
  - Renegade - Alacrity Support and Heals
  - Vindicator - Power Damage

---

### **= Medium Armor =**

- **Thief** - Thieves are the glass cannon's of Gw2, they hit the hardest but they also get hit the hardest. Thieves rely on mobility and dodging to stay alive.
  - Daredevil - Melee Power Damage (Think ninja)
  - Deadeye - Ranged Power Damage (Think sniper)
  - Spector - Condition Damage
- **Ranger** - Rangers have pets that help them fight enemies. Despite their name, rangers are also effective at melee combat.
  - Druid - Heals
  - Soulbeast - Power or Condition Damage
  - Untamed - Power or Condition Damage
- **Engineer** - Utility class that is quite proficient at applying conditions
  - Scrapper - Support condition strip and Barrier Heals
  - Holosmith - Power and Condition Damage
  - Mechanist - Condition Damage or support alacrity

---

### **- Light Armor -**

- **Necromancer** - Necromancers steal health from their enemies to heal themselves. This allows them to sustain a lot of damage while dealing damage.
  - Reaper - Power Damage
  - Scourge - Condition Damage or Barrier Heals
  - Harbinger - Condition Damage
- **Mesmer** - A unique class that uses clones to trick the enemy into attacking the wrong person (very effective in PvP).
  - Chronomancer - Boon Support or Power or Condition Damage
  - Mirage - Condition Damage or support alacrity
  - Virtuoso - Power Damage
- **Elementalist** - The most versatile class in Gw2 with the most skills. Very difficult to master, but very effective once mastered.
  - Tempest - Heal or Power or Condition Damage
  - Weaver - Power or Condition Damage
  - Catalyst - Power Damage

Keep in mind these are simply the most popular ways to play each class. The beauty of Gw2 is that you can make each class be whatever you like. Each class can do a little of everything, and with the appropriate build you can focus on a particular aspect to further develop in your character. After you've completed your character creation, you can name your character. You can only name your character something that someone else hasn't already used. You can use spaces, but no other special characters are allowed. Each letter after a space will be capitalized and only those letters can be capitalized.

# **Leveling**

Pretty much anything you do in Guild Wars 2 will give you experience (XP). The map in Guild Wars 2 is huge, so don't feel like you have to complete everything in an area before you move on. Here is a list of some of the best things you can do to gain xp:

- **[Renown Hearts](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Renown_Heart)**  
  Renown hearts are Gw2's version of quests. They tend to be a grind, but once completed will give you a lot of xp and some karma. Once you complete the heart, the npc that gives you the quest (with the heart over their head) will turn into a vendor that you can sell and buy stuff from with karma.

- **[Dynamic Events](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Dynamic_event)**  
  Dynamic Events will spawn randomly on the map. These can be completed by multiple people at the same time, so they are often more enjoyable than hearts. They will also give you a lot of xp and some karma for participating.

- **[Personal Story](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Personal_story)**  
  The events in your personal story are dependent on the choices you made when creating your character. You unlock a new chapter of your story every 10 levels. Doing your story will give you a ton of xp.

- **[Dungeons](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Dungeon)**  
  Once you reach level 30, you will start to unlock dungeons. You can gain about one level for every dungeon run. Dungeons are 5-man group content, so you will need a party to help you. You can either use the in-game Looking For Group (LFG) or find a group here in GWU. You will need to get the `Dungeoneer` notification role (see 📜dungeon-introduction for details) before you can ask in here though.

If you purchased the expansion it will come with a level 80 booster that will automatically boost your character to level 80. It is not recommended that you use this until you have at least reached level 30 since the levelling process will introduce you to the mechanics of your class and the game. You can also gain tomes of knowledge as rewards from PvP, which will give you one level each. You'll unlock access to PvP as soon as you reach level 2. While in PvP, your character will temporarily be boosted to level 80. This is also a good way to test out builds and see what your class will be like at level 80. However, again, this might be overwhelming for new players so it's not recommended.

# **After Level 80**

Congratulations, you made it to level 80! Now the real game begins. Most of the content in Guild Wars 2 is endgame content (after level 80). From here the world is yours, you can do whatever you want. Gw2 offers 3 different styles of gamemodes (PvE, PvP, and WvW) each of which has a treasure trove of goals and achievements for you to reach for:

### **[Player Versus Environment](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Player_versus_Environment)**  
PvE is the most popular gamemode which focuses on group content with players gathering in groups of 5, 10, 50+ to kill non-player character (npc) bosses.

- **[Fractals of the Mists](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Fractals_of_the_Mists)**  
  Fractals are 5-man group content recommended for starting getting into endgame PvE content.  

- **[Strike Missions](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Strike_Mission)**  
  Strikes are 10-man group content recommended for getting into raids.  

- **[Raids](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Raid)**  
  Raids are the hardest type of PvE content. It's recommended you start with Strikes or Fractals first.  

- **[Achievement Hunting](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Achievement)**  
  You can get achievements for everything in the game (even PvP and WvW). Achievement hunting is like Pokémon: *gotta catch 'em all*.  

- **[Farming, Trading, and Investing](https://www.gw2bltc.com/)**  
  By now you've probably discovered the Black Lion Trading Post. Believe it or not, Gw2 has its own fully functioning economy with fluctuating prices based on supply and demand. There are some people who make all of their money in the game by simply investing in materials and then crafting those materials into something they can sell for more.

---

### **[Player Versus Player](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Structured_PvP)**  
Guild War 2's action/tab hybrid combat system is one of the best out there. Practice and exhibit your mastery of combat in structured PvP (sPvP), where gear and teams are standardized, leaving only your builds, tactics, and raw button smashing supremacy to make you reign supreme.

- **[Conquest](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Conquest)**  
  Conquest battles are a 5v5 point capture game where your team fights to gain control of three points.  

- **[Automated Tournaments](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Automated_Tournaments)**  
  Auto Turnies are formal sPvP tournaments that happen every 2 hours.  

- **[Murderball](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Murderball)**  
  Murderball is a 5v5 team deathmatch where you fight to keep an orb in your zone for points.  

- **[Team Deathmatch](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Team_Deathmatch)**  
  Team Deathmatch is a 2v2 deathmatch where once you die, you cannot respawn.

---

### **[World Versus World](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/World_versus_World)**  
WvW is a game mode unique to Guild Wars 2. It is a form of PvP where you have 6 different servers (divided into 3 colored teams) fighting each other to control the map. This tends to get much more chaotic than sPvP as you frequently have groups of 100+ players all fighting each other.

- **[Commanding](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Commander)**  
  Lead large groups around WvW to capture, pillage, and dominate a map.  

- **[Zerging](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Zerg)**  
  Don't have a commander tag or don't want to tag up, but still want the thrill of large scale fights? You can always follow someone else's tag and join the zerg of bloodthirsty rioters.

- **[Roaming](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/World_versus_World#Roamer)**  
  Solo roam on a WvW map by running around and capturing camps.  

- **[Guild Versus Guild](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Guild_vs_Guild)**  
  A more organized form of WvW where guilds challenge each other to war.

No matter what you do choose to do, remember to always have fun and enjoy the game. Even though you're no longer a new player, you're still welcome here at the University. There is always more you can learn in a game as large as this.
