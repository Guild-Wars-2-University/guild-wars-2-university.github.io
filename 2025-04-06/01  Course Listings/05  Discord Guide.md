
# **What is Discord**

Discord is a free voice, video, and text chat app that's used by tens of millions of people to talk and hang out with their communities and friends. People use Discord daily to talk about many things, ranging from art projects and family trips to homework and mental health support. It's a home for communities of any size, but it's most widely used by small and active groups of people who talk regularly. These groups are called servers.

`Servers`

The vast majority of servers are private, invite-only spaces for groups of friends and communities to stay in touch and spend time together. There are also larger, more open communities, generally centered around specific topics such as popular games. Guild Wars 2 University is one of those public servers focused on teaching anyone how to play Guild Wars 2. Users can be a part of up to 50 servers at a time.

`Channels`

Every discord server has multiple channels. These channels are designed to separate conversations into different topics. At the top of each channel window, you can see a topic description for that channel.  
- **Text Channels** let users post messages, upload files, and share images for others to see at any time.  
- **Announcement Channels** are just like regular text channels except they can have their messages forwarded to other servers who follow them.  
- **Voice Channels** allow users to connect through a voice or video call in real time and can share their screen with their friends. There are also text channels associated with each voice channel for people on mute.  
- **Stage Channels** are a type of voice channel where only certain people are allowed to talk.  
- **Thread Channels** are a type of text channel where you can only send messages in threads.  

Many servers will also have an AFK voice channel. If your microphone does not receive any signal for 15 minutes, you will automatically get moved to the AFK voice channel. Our AFK voice channel is Detention Center.

`Roles`

In addition to different channels, servers also have different roles. Roles are categories which separate members of a server into different groups based on skill, interest, server rank, or server permissions.  
- **Role Permissions** allow users to do different things on the server depending on your roles. There are many different permissions, but the most notable are permission to view channels and permission to send messages in channels. There are some channels which are hidden from everyone unless they have certain roles. Likewise, there are some channels that only allow certain roles to send messages in. If you find that you cannot do something on a server, it's most likely because you do not have the appropriate roles to perform that action.  
- **Mention Roles** are roles which allow people to notify everyone with that role about relevant information: `@Student`. In order to ping a role simply type "@" and then the name of the role into a text channel. Not every role is mentionable. Typically, this is only allowed for interest-based roles. If you have received a ping discord will show a notification next to the server and channel you received the ping in and highlight the message in yellow for you.  
- **Role Colors** allow users to change the color of their display name based on the highest server role they have. Not every role will have a color attached to it. Typically, this is only used for server ranking roles.

# **User Interface**

Discord has a lot of fun features to help users communicate. Let's go over all the different features that you will run into while on a server.

`Messages Controls`

Each message that is sent in a text channel has a control bar that pops up if you mouse over it.

- **Edit a message** allows you to edit messages that you sent. You cannot edit attached files or stickers.  
- **Message Replies** are another way to organize conversations. This will attach a link to the replied message in your current message and notify the author you're replying to (optional).  
- **Threads** are temporary sub-channels that users can create to organize multiple conversations happening in the same text channel. Threads will become archived after a certain amount of time without anyone using them.  
- **Reactions** let you attach emojis to messages. Sometimes this feature will be disabled in certain channels. You can also add reactions that other people have added by clicking the emoji below the message. If you want to remove your reaction, simply click it again.  
  - **Reaction Roles** allow users to assign themselves roles by adding reactions to certain messages. They can also remove the role by removing their reaction.  
  - **Reaction Voting** is the unofficial polling system in Discord. Since reacting to messages also keeps count of how many people have reacted with certain emojis, reactions will often be used as a voting system.

---

`Channel Controls`

The bar at the top of the window allows you to control settings for the channel you're currently viewing.

- **Threads** let you easily see all the threads that are on the channel.  
- **Mute** lets you turn off notifications for unread messages on the channel.  
- **Pinned Messages** shows all important messages that were saved by administration on the channel.  
- **Search Box** lets you search the server for certain words or phrases in messages (you can filter by channel or author).

---

`Text Controls`

At the bottom of the window in a text channel you have permissions to send messages in, there is a text box. This is where you create and send messages in text channels.

- **Upload a File** to share videos, music, or documents with others. You can also drag and drop the file into the channel you want to share it in.  
- **Send a GIF** to open a popup that will let you search and select the perfect GIF for the conversation.  
- **Send a Sticker** to open a popup that will let you search for a sticker to attach to your message. Alternatively, you can type ":" and then describe the sticker you want. Stickers are like emojis but bigger and they can be animated.  
- **Add an Emoji** to open a popup that will let you search for an emoji to insert into your text. Alternatively, you can type ":" and then describe the emoji you want.

---



`Voice Controls`

If you join a voice chat you can interact with the voice channel in the bottom left side of the window. If you are playing a game, Discord will automatically detect your game and give you some extra features.

- **Join** a voice channel by simply clicking on the channel.  
- **Leave** your current voice channel.  
- **Mute** allows you to mute your microphone. Any input picked up by the mic will not be heard by others in the channel.  
- **Deafen** allows you to silence a voice channel. You will not be able to hear anything from the voice channel.  
- **Screenshare** allows you to show other users in voice chat your game window. This will also capture the audio from your game (and only your game). You can also share your entire screen by selecting **Screen** instead. This will not capture any audio.

# **Recommended Settings**

Your Discord account settings can be modified by selecting the **Settings** icon at the bottom left side of the window.

`Game Overlay`

Discord has an overlay for games that will show you who is talking in voice chat and allow you to preview notifications without leaving your game window. However, this has been known to make Guild Wars 2 run slower and sometimes crash. If you want to toggle the Discord overlay for games, go under `Activity Status` and click the **Overlay** icon next to the game.

---

`Edit Profile`

You can change your Discord profile under `User Profile`. From there, you can change your profile picture (avatar), banner color, and `About Me` to give a description of who you are.

---

`Input & Output`

- **Push to Talk** mutes your microphone unless you are pressing a button. By default, Discord will automatically detect when you are talking and turn your mic on. However, if you're in a noisy environment, it might be good to switch to push to talk.  
- **Input/Output Device** If you have multiple microphones or speakers hooked up to your computer, Discord may use the wrong one by default. Sometimes it can be good to manually pick which device to use.

# **Tips and Tricks**

There are a lot of other features that we did not cover here, but those were all of the most important ones. If you ever need help with Discord, feel free to ask someone how it works. Here are a couple of extra tips you may find useful before you go.

`Server Notification Settings`

There are some settings which allow you to have more control over your notifications on servers. Larger servers will normally suppress having every message notify you. However, that is an option if you want for smaller private servers. All these settings can be controlled by clicking on the name of the server in the top left side of the window.

- **Privacy Settings** controls whether people from that server can direct message you. Direct messages let you privately talk to a user by right clicking the user's name and selecting `Message`. By default, this is turned on. However, you can turn it off and prevent anyone on the server from privately messaging you.  
- **Notification Settings** There are two mention types which are restricted for administrative use. The first is `@everyone`: this will notify everyone on the server. The second is `@here`: this will notify everyone currently online in the server. If you want to turn off those notifications, you can toggle `Suppress @everyone and @here` (this will affect all channels). You can also turn off role notifications by toggling `Suppress All Role @mentions` (this will affect all channels). Alternatively, you can remove your notification role by removing your reaction in the top-most "notifications" channel.

---

`Markdown Text`

Markdown allows you to add some style to your messages. If you're not already aware, you can press `shift`+`enter` to make a new line without sending a message. This is also true with a lot of other messaging applications.  
- **BOLD:** You can make your words **bold** by adding `**` around it.  
- **_Italics:_** You can make your words _italicized_ by adding `*` around it.  
- **Underscore:** You can __underlined__ your text by adding `__` around it.  
- **~~Strikethrough~~:** You can ~~cross-out~~ your text by adding `~~` around it.  

> **Quote:** You can indent lines of text by adding `>` (for single line) or `>>>` (for multi-line) at the beginning of the paragraph.

- **Spoiler Text:** Discord also has a spoiler tag which allows you to ||block out some text|| in order to mark it as spoiler content by adding `||` around it.  
- **[Masked Links](https://example.com/):** You can add [links](https://example.com/) to Discord without the need to display the URL. Simply put the words you want to display in `[]` and put the URL link in `()` immediately after.  
- **`Timestamps`:** When coordinating events with people in different timezones, it can be helpful to use Discord timestamps to display the time in everyone's local timezone. For example: GW2 server reset is at 01:00. You can find a specific timestamp you want on [hammertime.cyou](https://hammertime.cyou/) or use the command `/timestamp`.

## **Headers**

You can create headers at three different levels by putting a `#`, `##`, or `###` at the beginning of your header line. Remember to add a space after the hashtags.

---

`Special Channels`

- **Channel Categories** separate channels into groups based on subject. You can collapse a category by clicking it and Discord will hide all the channels under that category. If you have an unread message in a category, it will display the channel even if the category is collapsed.  
- **UTC Clock** With so many users in different timezones in Guild Wars 2 University, we have a channel that acts as a clock at the top of the channels lists so that we sync our schedules for events: `Reset-2 | 22:15 - UTC`.  
  - `Reset!!! | 00:00 - UTC` is when daily reset happens in-game. Many people will give times based on that for scheduling: Reset-2 = 2 hours before reset, Reset+1 = 1 hour after reset.
