If you are looking to level up your crafting, it can be quite expensive. There is only one place to go in order to minimize the cost: https://gw2crafts.net/
This website looks at the current price of everything on the Trade Post in game and calculates the best way to maximize your crafting experience while minimizing the total cost of materials. Once you select a crafting profession, the page will list all of the materials you need and the order to craft items along with their recipes. **Refreshing the page will recalculate everything.** So once you start, do it all in one sitting.
