Crowd Control (CC) are effects that can be applied to enemies which temporarily disable the use of skills and/or movement. The "hard" CCs in this game are Daze <:D2_daze:768661512234467358>, Stun <:D2_stun:768661823989743705>, Knockdown <:D1_knockdown:768661618993004566>, Pull <:D1_pull:768661690405224448>, Knockback <:D1_knockback:768661585971249182>, Launch <:D1_launch:768661649917345812>, Float <:D1_float:768661549115506768>, Sink <:D1_sink:768661745417191424>, Fear <:C_fear:768642321973510175>, and Taunt <:C_taunt:768642374804439040>. There are also non-disabling condition which count as a "soft" CC: Blinded <:C_blinded:768642251420336138>, Chilled <:C_chilled:768642279287423017>, Crippled <:C_crippled:768642307121086534>, Immobile <:C_immobile:768642334085611530>, Slow <:C_slow:768642360325439488>, and Weakness <:C_weakness:768642416374710272>. There are other CCs in the game, but those are specific to certain class skills (ie. Mesmer's Signet of Humility which turns an enemy into a Moa). 

All of these CCs are the only thing that can affect a defiance bar (AKA "breaking" a break bar) which some Champion enemies have. A defiance bar shows up under a the health bar of an enemy when you target them. If the defiance bar is gray, it is locked and all CCs will have no effect on the enemy. If the defiance bar is blue, any CC applied to them will lower the blue bar instead of having the normal effect. Once the blue bar goes to 0 ("breaks"), the enemy will become stunned and take 50% more damage for 5 seconds while the defiance bar regenerates. "hard" CC lowers the defiance bar a substantial amount. "soft" CC prevents the defiance bar from regenerating if it hasn't been broken yet.
