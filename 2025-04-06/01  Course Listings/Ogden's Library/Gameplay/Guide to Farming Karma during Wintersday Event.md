# **Forward**
You can earn large quantities of karma in a relatively short amount of time during Wintersday through two sources, giving gifts to orphans scattered around Divinities Reach, aka [The Orphan Farm](<https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Orphan>), and [Motes of Candied Karma](<https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Mote_of_Candied_Karma>).  Motes of Candied Karma are rewards from Wintersday Daily Bonus chests, which are earned by doing specific activities, up to three times a day per activity.  These events are as follows:
- [Bell Choir](<https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Bell_Choir_Ensemble>)
- [Infinirarium](<https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Tixx's_Infinirarium>) and [Secret Lair](<https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Secret_Lair_of_the_Snowmen>) – Daily Bonus is done by completing either
  - Infinirarium is a dungeon
  - Secret Lair is a strike mission
- [Snowball Mayhem](<https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Snowball_Mayhem>)
  - Is a PvP arena
- [Snowfall Sprint Race](<https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Time_Trial:_Snowfall_Sprint>)
- [Toypocalypse](<https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Toypocalypse>)
- [Winter Wonderland](<https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Winter_Wonderland>)
  - Jumping puzzle

This guide will focus on receiving karma from the Orphan Farm and the Daily Bonus for the Bell Choir, Snowfall Sprint Race, and Winter Wonderland jumping puzzle.  The other Daily Bonus chests either take a long amount of time, or are inconsistent in the amount of time it will take you to finish them.  This makes them not worth accounting for in this guide as it is focused on quickly getting a bunch of karma each day, but not needing to dedicate your entire day to events to get said karma.

The best way to farm karma during Wintersday is to have a designated character for farming the karma each day.  Do all of your dailies on this character (or as many as you can) and then log off of them, thereby holding any remaining time on the buffs you have applied to that character.  If you complete some of the less reliable dailies, such as the strike mission, save the daily bonus chest until you are on your designated karma character to accept the reward.

The 2024 event is supposed to last from <t:1733850000:f>  to <t:1735837200:f>. That is 23 to 24 days of dailies.

# **Maxing Your Karma Bonus Percentage**
- Nourishment – [Super Mixed Parfait](<https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Super_Mixed_Parfait>) – 15%
  - Is kind of expensive for a food item.  There are other options that you can use that will give a 10% karma boost. A few 10% alternatives are:
    - [Ascended Feast](<https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Ascended_feast>) / [Birthday Cake Feast](<https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Feast_of_Delectable_Birthday_Cake>) if you find it on the ground
    - [Birthday Blaster](<https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Birthday_Blaster>) from the second birthday chest
      - Only lasts 10 minutes so need to be very diligent about reapplying the buff.
    - [Plate of Island Pudding](<https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Plate_of_Island_Pudding>)
      - Can be crafted or purchased from the Trading Post
- Enhancement – [Peppermint Oil](<https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Peppermint_Oil>), [Tin of Fruitcake](<https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Tin_of_Fruitcake>), or [Tuning Icicle](<https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Tuning_Icicle>) – 10%
  - Purchase from the Trading Post as it is significantly more expensive to craft.
  - As of time of writing, Peppermint Oil is significantly cheaper than the other options.
- [50% Karma Bonus](<https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/50%25_Karma_Bonus>) – 50%
  - Received from Karma Booster, Communal Boost Bonfire, Candy Corn Gobbler, etc.

  - Slotted in an ascended amulet.

# **Running the Karma Train**
Each day start by making sure you have all the buffs described above applied to your karma farming character.  From there: 

1. Head into the Bell Choir, and successfully do three rounds.  Do not accept the bonus reward chests at this point in time. You can accept the Gold event reward chests (the ones with the purple ribbon and gold medal).
2. Leave the Bell Choir.
3. Do one Snowfall Sprint Race race adventure.
4. Consume all of the chests from the events.
5. Head into the Winter Wonderland jumping puzzle, and successfully complete it 3 times.  Again, do not accept any bonus reward chests at this point in time.
6. Leave the jumping puzzle
7. Do the race two times
8. Accept the rewards
9. Do the Orphan Farm

I split up doing an instanced daily activity, and then doing the race because any Daily Bonus chests beyond 5, will automatically be accepted into your inventory once you change instances.  Additionally, because Bell Choir and Winter Wonderland are considered PvP instances, the karmic enrichment in the amulet does not work in those instances.  Thus you can miss out on some karma each day by not following the order shown above.

If you really want to maximize the duration of your karma buffs, you can do the jumping puzzle and bell choir on a separate character. Once done with either event, swap back to your karma farm character, do the race, and then accept the rewards.

# **Bell Choir**
The easiest way to do the bell choir is to have your fingers placed on the number row of your keyboard. This assumes that you still have all of the number keys set to their default key bind.

In the Bell Choir instance there are four platforms with three parts each; lower, middle, and upper part. Each part has a different number of notes to play in each song.  The lower part has the fewest notes, and the upper part has the most notes.

# **Winter Wonderland**
There are three paths that you can take. From easiest to hardest; the Quaggan Route, the Snowman Route, and the Gingerbread Route.  The starting platform for each path is north, central, and south respectively.  Standing on the starting platform for a path will provide you with a corresponding buff on your buff bar.

Each path offer the same amount of karma, so pick whichever path is easiest for you. The only reward difference is the number of Wintersday Gifts you receive from the chest at the end of the puzzle. You __**must**__ interact with the chest at the end of the puzzle to receive your rewards.

# **Orphan Farm**
I recommend having a pathing module open when doing the Orphan Farm to make it easier and faster.  Alternatively, you can use the [Orphan Map Image](<https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Orphan#/media/File:Orphans_map.jpg>) found on the Orphan page of the GW2 wiki.

1. Purchase 30 Toys from the [Charity Corps Seraph (Wintersday Skins)](<https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Charity_Corps_Seraph_(Wintersday_Skins)>). Each costs 504 karma each. Total 15,120 karma.
  - Alternatively, you can purchase the toys from the Trading Post.  As of time of writing, each one costs somewhere between 1 to 2 silver.
  - If you have the resources, purchase all of the presents you need for the Orphans for the entire duration of the festival to save time on the buff duration’s.
    - - Depending on when you are able to start doing the dailies on the first day of the festival, you will need to buy a total 690 or 720 toys.
    - - Total karma cost for buying all toys at once will be 347,760 to 362,880 karma.
2. Purchase 30 Rolls of Wrapping Paper from the [Charity Corps Serpah (Gift Wrapping)](<https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Charity_Corps_Seraph_(Gift_Wrapping)#vendor13>). Costs a total of 15,120 karma.
  - Like with the toys, if you decide to purchase all the gifts for the duration of the festival at once, total cost of the wrapping paper will be 347,760 to 362,880 karma.
3. Purchase the [Wrapped Gifts](<https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Wrapped_Gift>) from the Charity Corps Serpah (Gift Wrapping).
4. Head to the [<Dwayna Waypoint](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Plaza_of_Dwayna>). This is the waypoint next to the portal that leads to Queensdale.
5. Start Farming.

Something to note, is that Wrapped Gifts have a drop chance from killing enemies or opening containers during Wintersday.

# **Now What?**
If you finish and still want to farm karma, you have a couple of options. Farm the Winter Wonderland jumping puzzle, or the Bell Choir, whichever you prefer. This is also a good way to farm Wintersday Gifts.  Technically the Bell Choir will net you more karma faster.

If you want to prioritize getting Wintersday Gifts over karma, I recommend farming the jumping puzzle instead, especially if you are comfortable doing the Gingerbread Route.  This is because more likely than not, you will have to wait in between rounds of the bell choir.

Do be aware that since both instances are considered PvP instances, you cannot reapply food or utility items while inside.  If either wears off, you will have to leave the instance, or continue on without those buffs.  You can consider using primers to extend the duration of the buffs, before entering, to give you more time to farm.  This limitation does not apply to the candy cane buff.

# **Final Notes**
You should net a minimum of 118,260 karma from the Motes of Candied Karma and the Orphan Farm a day.
This assumes that:
- You have all of the buffs described on page 1 and 2
- Your karma bonus from achievement reward chests is 0%
- This excludes the Volunteer’s Blessing due to unreliability
- You are purchasing the toy skins for the Orphans with karma.

How much karma you receive from each round of Winter Wonderland, and each round of Bell Choir, will depend on if you are using your main karma farm character, or a separate character without all of the buffs to do the rounds.  Each round will provide a base karma of 1,000.
