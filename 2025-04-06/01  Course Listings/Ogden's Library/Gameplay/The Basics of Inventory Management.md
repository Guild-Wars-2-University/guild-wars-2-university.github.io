To successfully keep a sizable inventory you should have:
- Bag sizes of at least 15 per slot
- At least 1 Invisible bag filled with items you frequently use or drop from your main game mode, and are marketable, depositable, or salvageable
- Open all bags possible
- Ask yourself "do I really need that account bound thing?"
