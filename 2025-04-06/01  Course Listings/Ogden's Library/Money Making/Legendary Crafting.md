There's a lot of reasons players craft legendary gear in Guild Wars 2, despite it having no raw stat advantage over ascended gear. Some players are just infatuated by shiny things like the skritt are, while others like having the convenience that filling their legendary armory provides. Legendaries enable:
- Stat Swapping (Or Rune/Sigil Swapping For Legendaries Runes and Sigils)
- Easy Rune/Sigil Removal For Gear
- Use On Multiple Characters Simultaneously
- Epic Fashion To Impress All Your Friends and Lord Faren

Legendary crafting can be a large undertaking and will require a lot of time and gold committed for each piece of gear you make.
