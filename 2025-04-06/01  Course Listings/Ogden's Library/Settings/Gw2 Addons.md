	Guild Wars 2 has no official addon support and as such the use of addons is very restricted, with most people only using a DPS meter. That said, there are several addons, overlays, and plugins available which can provide a range of helpful extra features.
	
	💡 _Newer players, especially those who are yet to reach 80, are encouraged to play without addons such as pathing/marker packs or dps meters if possible, as they either trivialize content or are not necessary until attempting post-80 content._
	
	## <:4_anet:771126916341825536> [Policy on Third-Party Programs:](<https://help.guildwars2.com/hc/articles/360013625034>)
> 	ArenaNet does not review, approve,  or endorse any third-party program. Each use of such a program is made **at the account holder’s own risk**.
> 	Doing so [...] can lead to the **suspension or termination of your account** for violating the _Guild Wars 2_ Rules of Conduct.
	-# *Excerpt from [help.guildwars2.com](<https://help.guildwars2.com/hc/articles/360013625034>)*
	
	Summaries of the key points from the above policy, and macro policy, are included below. It is advised to have read and fully understood the article before using any third-party programs. 
	
	- Addons **must not** give a player an unfair advantage over another player.
	  - This includes providing information not normally visible or accessible to a player, for example skill cooldowns for swapped weapons, or automating trading post transactions.
	- Addons **must not** facilitate or promote unattended or automated gameplay.
	  - This includes macros for skill rotations.
	  - Macros *may* be used, but only for accessibility or re-binding. Generally, the rule is "one keypress = one action". Explicitly allowed exceptions are; a macro for dodge+jump, macros to play music using ingame instruments, and macros to quickly open stacks of items.
	
	### ⚠️ Use of any third-party program is done entirely at the account holder's own risk and may result in account termination.
	
	Some of the most commonly used addons include:
	- **[ArcDPS](<https://www.deltaconnected.com/arcdps/>)**
	  - A DPS meter for you and any party or squad members, extensible through addons to provide extra information such as healing stats or boon uptime.
	  - _💡 A more in-depth overview and setup guide for ArcDPS is available [here](<https://discord.com/channels/446550730640195586/1328506337503744141/1328506337503744141>)._
	- **[Blish HUD](<https://blishhud.com/>)**
	  - An overlay that provides many features through downloadable modules; the most popular being pathing routes & markers, and a customizable radial mount menu.
	- **[Gw2 Radial](<https://github.com/Friendly0Fire/GW2Radial>)**
	  - Adds radial menu which you can activate with a button press to quickly select a mount.
	- **Nexus**
	  - Similar to Blish HUD, Nexus provides downloadable modules with various functions and can be used to install and update ArcDPS among others.
	  - ⚠️ _**Tier 1, 2 & 3 Nexus addons, other than ArcDPS, likely fall outside of the acceptable use policy linked above and may result in account termination. Use at your own risk.**_

Guild Wars 2 has no official addon support and as such the use of addons is very restricted, with most people only using a DPS meter. That said, there are several addons, overlays, and plugins available which can provide a range of helpful extra features.

💡 _Newer players, especially those who are yet to reach 80, are encouraged to play without addons such as pathing/marker packs or dps meters if possible, as they either trivialize content or are not necessary until attempting post-80 content._
