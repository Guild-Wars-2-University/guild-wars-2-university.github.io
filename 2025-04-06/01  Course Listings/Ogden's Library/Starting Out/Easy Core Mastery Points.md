Masteries are where your xp goes after reaching Level 80, with different "tracks" for each expansion, including base game (called Core Tyria).
Here we will focus on this Core Tyria Mastery Track, as it contains some very interesting stuff, especially Autoloot, the ability to automatically get loot from enemies with a *huge* range without having to press F.
This one comes from the **Pact Commander** mastery track, and should be the first one to work on.

The second mastery track you should work on after that is the **Fractal Attunement** one, as it gives really good bonuses when doing fractals, which is a good way to earn gold (and experience !).
The third track in Core Tyria is related to legendary crafting, and if you're getting into that, mastery points are the least of your issue...

To unlock these masteries, you need mastery points. You might have a few already, but it is common for players to scramble a bit trying to get mastery points in Core Tyria.
But fear no more, now you will have a great resource to know where to get those points !

**The details are on this wiki page, maintained by yours truly: **<https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/User:Akala/Easy_Core_Mastery_Points>.
Note that there is an integrated tracker to grey out the ones you already have using an API key.
It is very detailed, but here is an overview of it.

First, the page was done without taking into account Living World Season 2.
While it does allow to get some easy mastery points, a lot of players don't have it, so I wanted to provide a guide that allows to get enough mastery points for both Pact Commander and Fractal Attunement without having to rely on those points from LWS2.
Here is a "short" summary of it:

---

**Hero Achievements**

"Hero" is a category of achievements, and among those there are some very easy ones to do for mastery points.
- 1 point is granted for reaching Lvel 80
- 8 points in total are granted for completing each of the 8 chapters of the Personal Story
- 1 point is granted for finishing all "Story Paths" of each dungeon (note that Arah is automatically done when you finish the Personal Story)

**Mastery Insights**

Mastery Insights are just places on the map a bit like Vistas, you get there, interact with a thing, and boom, you get a mastery point.
There are 9 of them in Core Tyria, *all reachable without glider nor mount*.
Feel free to look at the wiki page for more details on their locations.

**Hard (but actually easy) World Bosses**

Karka Queen and Tequatl are categorized as "Hard" world bosses (appearing on a set timer every day), but really, they're quite easy. Just show up there (a bit early for Karka Queen, she dies fast now) and follow the events, kill the big bad monster and boom, mastery point.

**Triple Trouble**

Triple Trouble is the third "Hard" world boss, and this one is actually hard.
It requires a good amount of coordination between three groups of players to defeat it, so it is not exactly something you can randomly do.
However on EU, we have a group of people running it *every day* and with an almost 100% success rate (details on the wiki page).
On NA the event is ran a bit more often, look in the LFG ingame to find groups for it when it's up.
You can get up to 5 Mastery Points from this one, 4 by just doing the event and killing each head, and a last one for doing a few more tasks.

---

**Easy but a bit long**

3 points can be obtained by doing some rather easy but a bit time consuming achievements.
Completing the Hidden Garden and Retrospective Runaround Jumping Puzzles will grant you one point for each.

The last one is from the Lion's Arch Exterminator achievement, a little hunt to kill karka hatchling all over Lion's Arch.
I strongly recommended at least following the wiki to help finding those, or even a marker pack with Taco/Blish HUD.

**Fractals**

Fractals are a big part of Guild Wars 2's endgame, and there are other places to explain how they work, but essentially, they're mini-dungeons, each with their own mechanics and enemies.
There are 10 Mastery Points that can be obtained rather easily in those, the list is on the wiki page.
Feel free to ask in https://discord.com/channels/446550730640195586/1164193460966916276 for a run to be organized to get these !

**Medium Difficulty Fractals**

4 more points can be obtained in Fractals, but are a bit more tricky to acquire.
These are the achievements "Fancy Footwork", "Flawless Fins Foil Foes", "Weapon Tester" and "Subject 6 Deep-Sixer".
They are not extremely hard but require some effort to get done. Again, feel free to ask for help to get these done.

---

**Not Very Hard but Very Annoying**

This last set of achievements is technically not very hard, but can be quite annoying to do for multiple reasons such as heavy RNG. I would not prioritize these, but if for example you already have some progress on some of them, or are farming Silverwastes, then maybe keep an eye out for them. 

- Koutalophile requires you to collect some spoons all over tyria. Most of them are easy to get, but a few can be tricky.
- Ruler of Shinies requires you to kill a specific boss in Dry Top, appearing only when the Sandstorm meta reached Tier 4. A bit random depending on how many people are there, but doable.
- Go for the Gold requires you to find 30 badges spread all over Silverwastes. A lot of these are quite annoying to reach, and while glider and mounts are not needed to do so, it surely helps.
- 5 more achievements/points require you to defeat Legendary ennmies in Silverwastes that spawn randomly. They are usually called out in map chat but not very reliable to get. Check out the wiki page for their location.

**About Living World Season 2**

As mentioned at the beginning of this page, this guide was designed to not have to rely on LWS2 for Mastery Points. However in case you do own LWS2, then you can easily get 8 points from just completing each chapter of the story.

Note that there are another 8 points from LWS2, the "episode mastery" achievements (e.g. Gates of Maguuma Mastery). These however require you to complete a bunch of other achievements to get them, including some of what is widely accepted as some of the hardest story instance achievements in the game, and as such are not recommended to pursue just for the sake of mastery points early on.
