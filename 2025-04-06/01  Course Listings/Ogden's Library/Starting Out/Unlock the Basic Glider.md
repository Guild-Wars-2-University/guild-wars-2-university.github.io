*No level requirement, End of Dragons expansion required*

1- To join our guild, post your In-Game Name.#### in the <#791874157720895498> channel. A staff member will send you an invite as soon as possible. If you are already a GWU member, you can skip to step 5.
2- Once someone sends you an invite, open your guild panel (default is "G"). 
3- At the bottom of the 5 guild slots, you should see `U N I V E R S I T Y [GWU]` (For <:NA:893819679234220032>) or `U N I V E R S I T Y Eu [GWU]` (For <:EU:893819679196479488>) with two option: Accept and Decline. Press on "Accept".
4- [optional step]  You can represent the guild by clicking on the check box at the left of GWU. This will give you 15% reduction on all your Waypoint travels.
5- On the top right corner of your guild panel, there is a button called Guild Hall. Press on it to be teleported to the Isle of Reflection.
6- Make your way to the Reflection Docks Waypoint `[&BPMMAAA=]` which is in the Northern part of the Isle. You can follow the road that leads north. 
7- When you arrive at the WP, walk on the dock all the way to the end where there is a portal with a boat on it. When getting closer to the portal, you will receive a pop-up that says "Returning to Shing Jea?", select "Yes."

You will then have the magnificent Seitung Province loading screen, at the end of which you will lend on a dock, with your brand new glider unlocked! 
Please note that the unlocked glider is the basic version and is account bound so you can use it on all characters.
