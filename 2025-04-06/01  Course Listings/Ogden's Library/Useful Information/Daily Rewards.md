Coming up with a daily gold routine is a great way to meet your gold needs without ever feeling like you NEED to grind for it. 

It's also a great way to get oriented in the game and find things to work toward so that you can expand the amount of gold you rake in each day. There's plenty of achievements and instanced content progression opportunities that will make you a lot of gold in the long run. The best part is that most daily gold earners have pretty consistent profits, so they rarely become obsolete or outdated. 

You can also team up with other [GWU] members and work on the requirements for some of these methods together!

**• Daily Tasks** - Complete any 3 tasks to gain 10 Achievement Points and 2 gold!  You can mix and match or do all 3 in a single category.
1) Open World has 4 options.
2) PvP has 3 potential options.
3) WvW has 3 potential options.

**• Crafting **
1) Lump of Mithrillium, Spool of Thick Elonian Cord, Glob of Elder Spirit Residue, Spool of Silk Weaving Thread each day as it can only be done once each day! ONLY do this if you have the materials. This is used towards crafting ascended gear!

**• Provisioner Tokens**
1) Buy your stuff: https://gw2lunchbox.com/ProvisionerHelper.html
2) Head to waypoints: `[&BN4HAAA=]`, `[&BNYHAAA=]`, `[&BMwHAAA=]`. You need 50 for 1 gift of craftmanship (and you need many) and that is needed for Legendary Raid Armor, Runes and Sigils. If you have a decent amount of gold, it will take 10 gold per day on average.

**• Pact Supply**
1) Pact Supply Network Agents (PSNA), type /wiki psna to see their current locations and copy the code in []. They sometimes offer expensive recipes at a huge discounted rate.

**• Keys**
1) Cache Keys - Buy 10 in Drizzlewood `[&BGQMAAA=]`.
2) Ornate Rusted Key - Kill 10 Krait `[&BLEEAAA=]`.
3) Buy 30-60 Fractal Encryption Keys `[Mistlock/Inside Fractal LA]` daily! 

**• Conversions/Daily rewards**
1) If you have the conversion tools and LOTS of bloodstone dust, dragonite ore and/or emphyreal star fragments, use: Herta, Princess, Star of Gratitude, Mawdrey II, Gleam of Sentience, etc. daily!
2) If you have Ley-Energy, Karmic, Fractal Reliquary, etc., it is recommended to check those out as well!

**• Guild Nodes**
1) Head to the guild hall and gather the nodes daily! Easy crafting resources!

**• Strikes**
1) There will be a strike that is "prioritized." Prioritized is ANET's fancy way of saying this is the daily strike. It rotates and changes each day!

**• Drizzlewood Materials**
1) Drizzlewood Material Donations `[&BGQMAAA=]`

1) Do all daily and recommended fractal at the highest tier you can work on. This is an easy source of gold (you earn more the higher you go).

**• Raids**

2) Watch video guides on wing bosses.
3) Practice against wing bosses.
