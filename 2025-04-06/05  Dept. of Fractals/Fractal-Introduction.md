# Fractal Roles
### T1 Fractals
This is the first tier of fractals and where you should start if learning fractals. No requirements for this role.
### T2 Fractals
You will need a minimum of 50 agony resistance and an understanding of the 22 different fractal dungeons to play at this level.
### T3 Fractals
You will need a minimum of 100 agony resistance to play at this level. It will also help if you have a good understanding of how to play your role in the party as coordination is key. 
### T4 Fractals
You will need a minimum of 150 agony resistance to play at this level. At this point you should have a full understanding of all dungeon mechanics, character skills, and role responsibilities. 
### CM Fractals
You have completed fractals on normal and are looking for training, progression, or clears of the CMs. Please only sign up for content you meet the requirements of.
