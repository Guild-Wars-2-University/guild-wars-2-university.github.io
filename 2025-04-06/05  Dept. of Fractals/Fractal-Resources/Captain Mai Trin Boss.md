Fractal Farming: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cs_21vrj9O0

Fractal farms are something that can be a very rewarding option, but definitely have their fair share of disadvantages. You'll likely want to be a relatively experienced tier 4 fractal player before you start doing these. They can also be extremely repetitive and often boring, depending on the type of player you are. If you do decide to try fractal farming, I **STRONGLY** recommend finding 4 friends or guildmates to do it with you and then joining voice chat to talk while you do it. Communicating about the farm is an obvious benefit to doing this, but it will also keep you entertained if you're able to have casual conversations with friends while you're farming.
