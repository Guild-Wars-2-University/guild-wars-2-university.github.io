**Nutshell guide:**
-DPS Mai Trin, kill echos that spawn, back to boss
-DPS Scarlett, when split happens, throw CC at CC echo, kill small on the way to the big one, kill big one, go back and CC last one
-Laser AoEs off group, try to stack them
-On big laser phase, the laser will line up with 2 circles, and one will be clear. Stand in clear one
