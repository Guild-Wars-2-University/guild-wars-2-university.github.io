**Nutshell guide:**
-1-2 people push orb into glowing floor, spawning adds at each dark circle in north, east, south, west. Kill adds, if they touch orb, giant shockwave downs everyone
-CC orb after it hits 2nd glowing circle
-Jormag spawns on north end, phase one just DPS
-Whoever gets AoEs, leave group, and stack them
-Once phase one dies, run to opposite side of arena to avoid instant kill attack
-Kill adds
-Phase 2 Primordus does small attack 2 or 3 times, then big instant kill attack again. Enough DPS skips
-Orb pushing
-Phase 3 Kralk
-Alternates between 2 trails of bad on the outside, and 1 in the middle. Start in middle, and lure all adds in and kill
-Move to outside once trails clear, and vice versa, until phase
-Kill another champion that spawns on east side, then more orb pushing, with death puddles that spawn under orb
-Phase 4 Mordremoth
-Everyone gets AoE, split up
-At the same time, Mordremoth face smashes the platform 3 times, dodge/jump/block the shockwaves. Aegis doesn't work
-Repeat splitting and jumping until boss dead
-Kill champion that spawns on east side
-More orb pushing
-Phase 5 Zhaitan
-3 champion giants spawn, CC fast and kill. They have death puddles and fear everyone, so stability is nice
-Kill champion that spawns in west, and push another orb
-Phase 6 Soo-won
-DPS to 50%, avoiding shockwaves and a bunch of orbs circling platform
-Co-orb-inated push. Orb spawns in middle, shooting out more death orbs in every direction, and adds spawn
-Rate of attack pushes orb faster, not damage dealt. Keep orb as close to middle as possible. If the orb starts moving away from you, just stop attacking, and let the other side cancel its momentum, and you can cleave adds
-Once orb is dead, DPS Soo-won again. You can ignore the champion dragon add thing and just health through its attacks
-Win
