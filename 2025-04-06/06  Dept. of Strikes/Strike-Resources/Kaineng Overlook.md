**Nutshell guide:**
-Hit boss, zipline up to actual fight as a group
-Don't stand in front of boss when he's about to dash
-Numbers get off group, stand with group on one side of boss, numbers on opposide side of boss, with space between them, phase at 66%
-Ritualist/mindblade/enforcer. Someone hits mindblade, pulls to ritualist to cleave. Different person makes sure to stand near enforcer once he finishes dancing to get aggro, then pull away from group
-Once ritualist and mindblade dead, focus enforcer
-Zipline back to upper platform
-Same as first phase, plus an aoe split phase every time he changes corners. People with yellow separate from group, and dodge when yellow circle about 3/4 full to avoid an attack
-Mech and sniper: have one sub focus sniper whenever it's on the platform, then go back to mech to keep their HP less than 20% different
-Numbers make sure to stand next to mech so it doesn't jump around a bunch
-Zipline back to boss. More of the same, but he dashes the opposite direction this time
