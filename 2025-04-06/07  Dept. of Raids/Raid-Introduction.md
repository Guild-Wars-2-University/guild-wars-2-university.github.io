# Welcome to Raids!
Here at GWU, we pride ourselves on creating a positive environment for learning and improving yourself. We have a variety of knowledgeable commanders who will be able to answer any questions you might have and provide feedback on areas that went well, and things that require improvement. So please ask questions if you have one, and remember that we are all learning together.

## Raiding Rules:
```md
1. If you sign up for a raid event, *we expect you to show up*. If you sign up and later realize you can't make it, you need to unregister from the event. If the squad composition has already been posted please inform the organizer as soon as possible.
2. Have a general understanding of your class, what role you are filling, what your CC skills are, and appropriate gear (exotic or higher) for your role.
3. Be polite and courteous to the rest of the squad, do not talk over commanders when they are explaining things, and be respectful throughout the raid.
4. If you need help preparing to raid or with your build/gear, please ask in #raid-discussion, talk to one of our Raid Commanders or other staff, or check out build guide sites such as Snow Crows, Metabattle, etc.
5.
```

# Raid Roles
### Raid Training
Trainings are available to all interested parties. The only requirements are 'What we expect from you' above.
### Raids
You have trained on or completed raid bosses. Progression runs contain minimal explanations. Clear runs contain no explanations. Please only sign up for content you meet the requirements of.
