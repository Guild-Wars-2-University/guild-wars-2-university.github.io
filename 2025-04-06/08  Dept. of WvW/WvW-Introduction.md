# Welcome to WvW!
WvW is one of the “endgames” of GW2, but because of the horizontal gear grind nature of the game you can hop in and get your feet wet as soon as you have a level 80 character. You can access WvW by opening the scoreboard menu (default bind is B), or clicking the castle icon at the top of the game window.

## What is WvW?
**The general goal:** Defend your structures, siege and capture enemy structures, and kill the enemies!
This game mode is played primarily in 2 ways: large-scale zergs and small-scale roaming.
- Zergs are generally 15 or more players which group up to fight other large groups of enemies and take heavily fortified structures. In zergs, fights are often won by positioning of the groups and burst damage from your group as a whole. For this reason, make sure you stand as close to your commander as possible and join their voice communication if they have it, as they will usually be where the damage isn’t and calling when to put out your burst damage. Zergs rely heavily on support players to keep everyone happy and healthy.
- Roaming is usually done in small groups of 1-5 players, where each player brings a more selfish build and there are no pure supports. These groups focus on taking unfortified supply camps, skirmishing with other roaming groups, and creating havoc on the map by taking large structures on the map unnoticed.
