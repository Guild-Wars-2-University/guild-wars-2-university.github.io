The Zaishen Order Hall is a server intended to be a relaxed learning environment for those looking to scrim other GW2 players. Our philosophy for these scrims are the following:

● Balanced Teams: Far too often GvGs, Scrims, and In-Houses can become a one-way stomp. Commanders will be incentivized to balance the teams as much as possible mid-scrim.

● Patience: We want to encourage more players to enter the GvG scene. Inexperienced players might be coached mid-scrim. We appreciate everyone's patience while we take a few minutes between rounds to accomplish this.

● Support: We want to do our part to support the NA GvG community. Doing everything we can both inside and outside the Field, such as different types of trainings and vod reviews, to grow player skill and comfort so we can ultimately network them with a GvG guild.

**Community Rules - Inside and Outside of the Hall**

1. Absolutely no hateful content directed towards any person or group. This includes racist, ableist, homophobic, transphobic, and anti-Semitic slurs.
2. Leave toxicity and elitism at the door. This is a fun & relaxed learning environment.
3. Treat others in the community with respect regardless of skill level.
