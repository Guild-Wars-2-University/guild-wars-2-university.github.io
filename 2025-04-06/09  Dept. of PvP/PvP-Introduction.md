# Welcome to PvP!
While PvP is a smaller aspect of the game, it's one of the best ways to get acquainted with the combat system and learn some of its quirks. It's also a ton of fun and GW2 has a high skill ceiling when it comes to winning both your 1v1 and your PvP games. 
There is standardized gear in PvP, so you're always on a level playing field, even with a brand new character. **You can PvP starting from level 2!** All traits and skills are available for all characters, regardless of level and hero points, assuming you own the expansion for the respective elite specialization.

Any player looking to try out PvP (or get better) can contact <@300712435478953984>, as PvP classes are generally 1 on 1 where he can give focused feedback and arrange appointments (not scary, he just need to set aside time). Players coming from other PvP-only games will find the PvP community here generally less toxic than other games, but there will always be bad apples. No shame in filling up your block list.
