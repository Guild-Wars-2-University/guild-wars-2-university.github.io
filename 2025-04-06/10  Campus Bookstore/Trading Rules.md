**Safe Trading Advice**
We want all of our traders to have the best trading experience possible. Here's a list of things you can do to practice safe trading and limit the risk of being scammed.

**Before the Trade**
• Cross-reference their account name with the <#767063895973298257>. If they're on it, do not trade with them and report their discord name to a <@&735975417260802109>. 
• Check if they are a <@&854379171438788668> or check their trade rep on r/GW2Exchange or Overflow Trading Company discord server. 
• Check their achievement points. Low AP is a big red flag. 
• Ask for an API key to verify they have the item you're trading for. 
• Ask for a <@&855975952801464342> to act as a intermediary for the trade. 
• Use your gut. If anything seems off, call it off, you can always find someone else to trade with.

**During the Trade**
• We strongly recommend you record, or at least screenshot, any trade with someone you've never traded with before or any big-ticket trades. 
• Hop into <#854072430079508550> with the other player and invite them to a party in-game. 
• In-game, go over the details of the deal and what exactly you will be trading for what. Have the other player agree and screen-share while working on conversions and crafting. 
• Make sure to get their account name included somewhere in the recording/screenshots.

---

**If You Are The Victim of a Scam**
• Collect all the information and evidence you have about the incident.
• Use the command `-ticket open Trade Scam` in <#854066871342465025> to open a private ticket with a <@&735975417260802109>. You'll be asked to provide us with what happened, any screenshots/recordings, their in-game name, their discord name if you know it, and what was scammed. The more evidence you have the better. 
• At the same time, you need to submit a ticket to ArenaNet Support. Again you'll need to provide your account of what happened, any evidence and what was scammed. All discord evidence will be ignored as it is not verifiable by ANet.

**Standard Trading Rates**
Here are some of the standard rates for common trade transactions. You are not restricted to follow these rates, but if you want to ensure a fair trade it is recommend you do.

• *Individual items = 90% current TP sell value (not including TP tax)*
• T5 -> T6 material conversion = 40 gold per set (140 spirit shards)
• Gift of Mastery = 500 gold or 900 gold if mystic clovers are included 
• Raid Instance Openers = 5 gold per instance
• Fractal Encryptions = 50 gold per stack
• Stabilizing Matrices = 70-75 gold per stack
• Glob of Ectoplasm = 50-55 gold per stack
• Mystic Coin = 2-2.2 gold each
• T6 materials = 360-380 gold per set of stacks

If you're unsure of what an item should be sold at for a fair price, ask a <@&855975952801464342> for help in <#736237424627417179>.
There is a 500 limit weekly on how much gold you can collect through the mail in-game. Because of this it's common for people to use other forms of payment. The most common forms of payment are Mystic Coins, Globs of Ectoplasm, and T6 materials. If you're requesting a middleman for a trade, it's polite to use one of these alternative forms in order to not use up their gold limit.
