# Intro to Fractals of the Mists 

Now welcome to the real Guild Wars 2. With your max level character you're going to want to get the best gear, piles of gold, and (most importantly) lots of friends to play with. Look no farther. Dessa's new Fractal Project is looking for able-bodied adventurers to delve into the past and unlock it's secrets. In return you may keep anything you find while exploring the mists. Together we will learn, guide, and conquer each Fractal level, both normal and challenge mode. I am excited to begin this journey with Guild Wars 2 University!
