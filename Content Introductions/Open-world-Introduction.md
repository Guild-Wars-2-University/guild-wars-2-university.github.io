# Intro to Open World
Welcome to Guild Wars 2 PvE (Player vs Environment)! As soon as you've finished the tutorial, you can start exploring and discovering what the open world of Tyria has to offer!

You may come across other players in the world, and they will generally be helpful. Don't worry about kill-stealing; anyone that hits an enemy will get loot and XP for the kill.

Some of the many ways to PvE include:
- **Personal Story:** You will unlock a new chapter every 10 levels. Once you've finished the final chapter, expansions will offer you new stories and new places to explore!
- **Events & Metas:** Every map has a ton of different events, and many maps have group events that will require teamwork to complete.
- **Farming:** Farmtrains are a great way to earn lots of loot for your account. You can often find these groups in the in-game LFG.
- **Achievement Hunting:** There is a near-infinite amount of achievements in this game, and new achievements get added all the time. You can find these in your Hero Panel.
- **Guild Missions:** Join us every week for Guild Missions, where there will be races, puzzles, bounties, and socializing! (Don't worry, we're friendly and chill.) You'll be rewarded with Guild Commendations, which you can trade for gear, cosmetics, and other cool stuff!
