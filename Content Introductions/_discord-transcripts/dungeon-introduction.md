  # Intro To Dungeons 
  Welcome to instanced group content! Dungeons are accessible to players starting from level 30 up to max-level and have their own storyline that ties into the player's Personal Story, offering multiple paths with puzzles and combat encounters. You can team up with friends or meet new people through the LFG system to enjoy this content at your own speed.

  **Getting There:** You can access all Dungeons through the [Dungeon Transportation](<https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Dungeon_Transportation>) at Fort Marriner in Lion's Arch. The nearest waypoint is `[&BDAEAAA=]` Fort Marriner Waypoint.

  Two ways to discover dungeons:
  - **Story Paths:** Introduces you to the members of Destiny's Edge as an optional part of your Personal Story and the different cultures of Tyria through cutscenes and combat encounters.
  - **Explorable Paths:** Unlocks after completing a dungeon's story path with a character or accessed by joining a player who has it unlocked. They follow further storylines taking place after your initial visit to the dungeon for more research and problem-solving. Some of them require to level up before unlocking them.

  *If you've been part of official Dungeoneering groups organized by GWU, we would love to hear from you! Check out [our Dungeons feedback form](<https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdkbCT1JUVspXNTOd31psXlb_dpiFhI6wCk4E14U_oOzzfW_w/viewform?usp=sharing>) and __share your ideas on how we can improve our events__ or tell us what you thought about them as they are.*
