**__Unidentified Gear__**
All endgame content has shifted to dropping unidentified gear instead of random items (my inventory thanks you), but this has lead to a change in how you should best process your hard earned loot into cold hard cash. The rarity of what you open when you crack unids is not affected by magic find, so the TP price is about equal to what the value of the gear is. That said, there's 2 ways about it: Open or Sell.

---

This is a general salvage guide that applies to more than just unids
When you open gear, you generally salvage it as follows: white/blue = basic/copper-fed, green = runecrafter's (so long as it has a rune/sigil attached, some fractal drops do not), yellow = mystic/silver-fed or sell if worth more than 1 ecto (only applies to Lv. 68+, otherwise almost always sell it, or salvage if the TP value is negligible [under-leveled rare gear is in a weird spot]), exo = MSK/BLSK/Sell depending on the price and the price of the rune/sigil affixed to it.) 
https://fast.farming-community.eu/salvaging/unid-gear

---

Now the other way is to sell it. BUT! Do NOT sell it to the trading post. As I hope I've informed all of you, the TP takes a 15% tax on all items sold, paid by the seller. But, the VALUE of that gear is defined by the TP, so why should you get 85% of the value of your loot? Enter the magic of Peer-to-peer trading, or trust-based mail trading. A TP baron (me) will pay 95% of the TP value for that gear, and profit 5% for our time spent processing.

**__Champion Bags and Boxes__**
Opening champion bags on the level described by https://fast.farming-community.eu/bags/bag-opener for the greatest profits. Why is this? Because it scales the levels of gear and items you can get, which vary in value depending on level.

**__Fractal Encryptions & Ascended Ring drops__**
You should always buy your deeply discounted keys. ALWAYS! even if you have to buy encryptions off the TP, it's still profitable. Free daily money sitting here, don't let it slip past!
What about those pesky rings with garbage stats? Salvage them! But as per usual, there's a catch. It is high recommended that you attune your rings (maybe 25s cost) to double your salvage yield. As for buying the salvage kits, they're all about the same price (raid, fractal, wvw, pvp merchants), but get a 25 kit.
