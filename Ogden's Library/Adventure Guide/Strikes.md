# Strikes

> Strike Missions, or strikes, are 10 player squad-based PvE instances for level 80 characters. Unlike raids, strike missions are repeatable single bossing encounters of characters encountered during the story with daily rewards. Each encounter typically involves multiple phases with several unique mechanics to test squad coordination, damage output, positioning, and player builds. Encounters may also include an enrage timer where the encounter fails when time expires.
> 
> -# [Strike Mission - GW2 Wiki](<https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Strike_Mission>)
