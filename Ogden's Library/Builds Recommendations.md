If you're looking for good builds, there are a variety of different websites that offer endorsed builds for different playstyles.

---

• Snow Crows is a renowned raiding guild. They have builds that are good for group PvE content: raids, fractals, and dungeons.
<https://snowcrows.com/builds>

---

• Discretize is a EU guild that focuses on fractals. Their builds are made specifically with fractals in mind. They also have amazing guides. 
<https://discretize.eu/builds>

---

• If you're looking for different types of builds or maybe considering publishing some of your own. You can also access a variety of other types of builds which are posted and rated by the Gw2 community on Meta Battle. Their Open World, WvW and PvP builds are particularly great.
 <https://metabattle.com/wiki/MetaBattle_Wiki>
