As you continue playing the game, you will begin to gather all kinds of strange currencies that don't look like money if you have no other use for them, or have already gotten all you want from them (LWS4 currencies post skyscale, Eternal ice shards, karma, daily competitive potions) can be turned into liquid gold with a little effort and some crafting. Below are a set of tools for converting some currencies:

---

**Saga Currencies:** Trade for LWS4 currencies and consume for Volatile Magic <:5_volatile:779525205071757312>

---

<:5_volatile:779525205071757312> **Volatile Magic:** Trade for shipments according to https://gw2lunchbox.com/IstanShipments.html

---

<:5_unbound:779525164185681931>**Unbound Magic: **Buy and open Magic-Warped Bundles from vendor in Ember Bay

---

<:5_karma:779526262535356426> **Karma:** Trade caches or mystic forging and selling depending on how many trader's keys you have, refer to gw2efficiency for specifics https://gw2efficiency.com/currencies/karma

---

**Competitive format daily potions:** sell to guild halls for their upgrades (join guild, deposit them, get paid). Rates are 50s/PvP potion and 80s/WvW potion.

---

<:5_spirit_shard:779525086130339900> **Sprit shards: **Either use them for crafts or material promotions, rates according to gw2efficiency:
https://gw2efficiency.com/currencies/spirit-shards -- Be mindful of how you have price calculations set, many items are not sold very often and you can get yourself some shiny weapons (worth all your money) stuck on the TP if not careful!
Alternatively you can sell them to other players for direct conversions for 42.85s/shard. Lots of clicking but good money. See `Gift of Mastery Trades` thread for more information.
