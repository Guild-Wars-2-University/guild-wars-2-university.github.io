Gold is the most used currency in the game, and there's a good chance that if you're trying to farm a material or drop, it's faster to farm gold and then buy it.

---

https://www.peuresearchcenter.com/ is the place to go for farming data, with details on each farm, and estimated profits. The specific profits of a farm are located on the benchmarks page. Right now, the most profitable farms are gathering in Lake Doric (with gathering glyphs that you won't have so I'm ignoring this), the Bitterfrost Frontier farm (a forgotten LWS3 map that has managed to hold up), and Drizzlewood Coast, from the new Icebrood Saga.

General benchmarks for a "good" gold farm are making ~15g/hr. To achieve this, see the "about benchmarks" page for specific account unlocks and boosters which will help.

---

The main things to ensure success in a gold farm are to stick with the commander, and tag as many events and mobs as possible for max loot. It is also very important to have maxed out the masteries of the world zone you're in so the exp turns into spirit shards, which is more gold in your pocket.
When it comes to finding farms, there are usually ample groups in LFG, just pick the map you want!
Happy farming and may riches be upon you!
