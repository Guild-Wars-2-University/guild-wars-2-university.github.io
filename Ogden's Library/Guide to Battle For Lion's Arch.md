# Battle For Lion's Arch

> The Battle For Lion's Arch is a public instance zone in Kryta. It represents the destroyed and hostile captured Lion's Arch during the The Battle for Lion's Arch story step in Living World Season 1, and it's based on the zone Lion's Arch (Enemy Controlled) from the original Season 1 releases. Although this instance is used for a story step, it is a Public Instance and can also be entered while not on the related story step.
> 
> -# [The Battle For Lion's Arch](<https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/The_Battle_For_Lion%27s_Arch>)

## Part 1: Recapture Lion's Arch
The beginning of the event starts with capturing 12 districts of lion's arch. Each district has a waypoint associated with it, but initially only 3 will be available. Once completed, each of the initial districts will unlock all of their corresponding waypoints for adjascent districts.

### Tips
- It is best to split up into three squads for this part of the event. 
	  - Blue Squad
	    - Melee Damage Preferred
	    - Start at Priory Foothold (Eastern WP)
	    - Complete Eastern Ward, Inner Harbor, and Postern Ward districts
	  - Green Squad
	    - Ranged Damage Preferred
	    - Start at Commodore's Quarter (Northern WP)
	    - Complete Grand Piazza, Western Ward, and White Crane Terrace districts
	  - Red Squad
	    - Condition Damage Preferred
	    - Start at Bloodcoast Ward (Southern WP)
	    - Complete Bloodcoast Ward, Farshore Ward, and Lion's Gate districts

## Part 2: Fighting the Watchwork Knights

After capturing the 12 districts, a 1 minute timer will start until the Watchwork Knights appear. You have 15 minutes to kill all 3 knights, which require between 7 and 15 players each. Less than 7 players and you will lack the DPS and break bar damage to complete it within the required time. The knights must be killed within 1 minute of each other, or they will respawn with 33% health and the fight will continue until the 15 minutes are up.

### Mechanics

- Each knight has unique abilities, but in general they cycle through similar patterns.
- Each knight will auto attack in some manner between larger attacks, it is unknown how it decides who it targets, but the damage is minimal.
- Each knight will perform a large scale aoe attack that requires you to stand close to the knight to avoid.
- Each knight will perform a large scale aoe attack and become immune to damage, but exposes it's break bar.
  - Breaking the defiance bar will trigger the exposed condition, but also the aoe attack will invert, meaning those who were in a safe zone are now in danger. You will most likely need to dodge.
  - This attack is an instant trip to the down state.
  - The window to break the defiance bar is about 10 seconds.
  - The amount of break bar damage required is unknown, but I estimate it in the 3000-4000 range. 
  - Breaking the defiance bar will trigger it's corresponding prismatic color to appear at each fight.
- Prismatic color fields (red, green, blue) will appear during the fight, walking through them grants you a buff.
  - Collecting colors that do not match the knight you're fighting increases your damage by 25% against it, up to 50% total.
  - Collecting all three colors gives you a white Prime Light attunement, increasing your damage by 25% against all knights. This a dps decrease if you have the two correct colors.
  - Collecting the color that matches the knight you're fighting decreases your damage by 25% against it.

### Tips
- Watch for calls to slow and stop damage by the commander.
- Automatically stop damage at 10% and wait for coordination with the commander.
- Pick up downed players if they are close.
- Respawn at the closest WP if you are dead, do not wait for resurrection.
- Pick up all three colors if you accidentally collected the wrong color. +25% is better than -25%.
- Collect two opposing colors for a 50% total damage increase.
- The more break bar damage you bring, the easier this fight becomes.
- If all of the knights are at 10% or less, and your knight dies, you can waypoint and go help another group kill their knight.
