# Festival Guide: Lunar New Year Festival
Begins usually sometime between **late January to early February** and lasts for four weeks.

-# ~~                    ~~

Lunar New Year is the new year festival in Guild Wars 2, and it makes use of customs from Chinese, Korean, Thai and other East Asian Lunar New Year festivals. This video is a little outdated as the magic find cap is lower these days. But the basics are correct. Please see the wiki for [up to date magic find values](<https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Magic_Find>)

If you’re trying to prepare, guild commendations are a huge help. I can be talked into extra runs if the normal NA and EU times are not good for you, but the fewer people we have the fewer missions we can do. So if you want this *please* ask in advance! Obviously you must be in the in game guild for this to work!

You may wish to save up some luck starting about now. This works best if you have bank space or an artificer mule. Don’t stress out about making huge stacks, but saving a little can be nice.

A lot of lunar new year things cost gold in increments of 8g 88 silver because this is an auspicious number. Plan ahead if you expect to want lots of shiny things. Now is a great time to start doing daily Tequatl or daily fractals if you know you love shiny things. Also… it’s the year of the dragon. If you love dragons more than anything, be prepared.

This one is doable at level 2, but due to the gold values involved I’m not *as* comfortable saying do this one at level 2 and free to play as I would be for other festivals. There’s a lot you can do! Preparing in advance works! But this one may feel more limiting.
