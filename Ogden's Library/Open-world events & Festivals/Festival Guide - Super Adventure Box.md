# Festival Guide: Super Adventure Box
Begins usually sometime between __late March and mid April__ and lasts for four weeks.

> *"Super Adventure Festival's main attraction is the annual opening of Moto's Super Adventure Box in March and April.*
> 
> *Originally created for April Fools' Day 2013, [Super Adventure Box](<https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Super_Adventure_Box>) is a special dungeon focused on old school platforming, with pixelated graphics and retro soundtrack, designed for any party size. Baubles and various coins can be gathered inside to exchange them for holographic weapon skins and other rewards."*
