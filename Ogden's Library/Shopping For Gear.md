Okay, so you've picked out your dream build, now you need to put it together. This thread covers how to do that in a cost effective manner.

---

**__Any stat armor__**
If you're someone who plays PvP or WvW, you'll get Warlord's Armor Boxes. Those are anystat exotic armor pieces of your choice. Use them. Additionally, if you're after that fancy 4 stat gear that's pricy on the TP, you can check the price of your desired armor against Shoulders of the `Ebon Vanguard Choice Chest` (shoulders), `Ice Golem's Maw Box` (helmet), or `Blood Legion Soldier Helm Box` (Charr-specific helmet).

---

**__Buying from the TP__**
Filter by stats using the gear beside the search bar, because if you type the prefix in you'll only find crafted gear, which is often much more expensive. Do this for remaining armor, weapons, and trinkets.

**__Ascended gear__**
If you're a PvPer, combine all 4 Wings of Glory in the Mystic Forge for the cheapest backpiece available.
Otherwise, get all trinkets through LWS3 for winterberries and unbound magic, possibly getting Icebrood Saga trinkets if you have a lot of karma and regularly do strikes.
For armor and weapons, go through the gamemode you frequent the most as that's the most painless and ultimately the most effective. Also make sure to stat swap any random drops you get to something useful (weapons and armor only), do `/wiki stat swap` in game to bring up the guide on that.

---

**__Stats Selectable Exotic Armor__**
There are a few Armor Boxes that can be gathered throughout the world, which contain pieces of armor with selectable stats. In my opinion, Bladed Armor Boxes are the easiest to farm. 
In Heart of Thorns' first map (Verdant Brink), you can complete events to receive Crowbars, with which you can pry open Airship Cargo and Large Airship Cargo, scattered around the map. These will contain Airship Parts, which is VB's currency. 

To unlock the vendors who sell `Bladed Headgear Box`, `Bladed Shoulders Box`, `Bladed Glove Box`, `Bladed Leggings Box` and `Bladed Boots Box`, you need to train Itzel Language in the Itzel Lore Mastery Track. (Second point)

To buy all 5 pieces of gear, you need 2500 Airship Parts and 5 gold. (Both currencies are account wide)

Once you obtain the box, make sure you open them on the character that needs the piece, for it becomes soulbound upon opening. You can then choose Light, Medium or Heavy armor. Next step is simply selecting your stats of preference. 

To obtain the Bladed Coat Box, you need to complete Verdant Brink's Night Meta at Tier 4 with a 100% participation. https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Bladed_Armor_Box

Here are some other ways to acquire easy to access stats selectable gear. 
**Exotic**
* [TEID gear](<https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/TEID-07_Unified_Vendor_System) (WvW vendor, but you may still have some of that currency even if you've never set foot into WvW)

**Ascended**
* [Wizard's Ascended Armor Chest](<https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Wizard%27s_Ascended_Armor_Chest>) (ascended, 3 armor and 1 weapon per season)
All ascended gear can be [stat swapped](<https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Stat_changing>) if it's not the stats you want.

Note that for all stats selectable, you need to _own the content from which it's from_ to have access to the stat.
