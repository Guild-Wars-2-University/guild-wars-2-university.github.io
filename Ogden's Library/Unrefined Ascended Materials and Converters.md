There are 3 main materials, Bloodstone Dust (from champion bags), Dragonite Ore (Large Chests and World Bosses), and Empyreal Fragments (Small Chests). These materials are refined and then used to create various ascended gear, but you will most likely find yourself with a large surplus of inventory hogging-seemingly valuable items. "What oh ever do I do?!" you might ask. Turn them into loot bags, of course! Converters (also gobblers or eaters) are gizmos which you can feed these ascended materials to and reward you with shiny loot bags to crack (on your bag opener character as described by https://fast.farming-community.eu/bags/bag-opener for max $$ - more on that later). Here is a link to them: https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Converter.

---

Many converters are linked to achievements, so I recommend spending some time to work on them, guides are available for all, and well worth the effort, from inventory space alone! Each converter can eat from 150 to 300 of a resource per day, depending on the converter and your RNG. There are also converters for other commonly hoarded currencies like the HoT map currencies and karma (although the karmic converter is a bit of a pricey achievement).
