# Websites and Tools

This resource serves as a collection of useful websites and tools to assist you with various Guild Wars 2 activities.
---
---
## General Tools
- Guild Wars 2 Wiki: <https://wiki.guildwars2.com>
	- The official wiki resource for the game, maintained by community contributors. The wiki has _extensive_ information about everything in-game. When in doubt, check the wiki!
	- **Note:** You can directly open the wiki by typing `/wiki` or `/wiki <search term>` you can even shift-click an item or map objective after typing `/wiki` to search for the object directly.
- Guild Wars 2 Efficiency: <https://gw2efficiency.com>
	- A website that serves as the 'Swiss Army Knife' of Guild Wars 2 tools, such as tracking account value, tracking achievements, character inventories and bank items, timers, statistics and crafting calculators.
- Gw2 Timer: <https://gw2timer.com>
	- Timers for map meta events, world bosses and more.
* gw2lunchbox: https://gw2lunchbox.com
    * A collection of smaller tools that help with trading amongst other things.

## Trading Post Tools
* Guild Wars 2 Black Lion Trading Company: https://www.gw2bltc.com
	* Database and search engine for current and historical trading post prices.
* GW2TP: <https://www.gw2tp.com>
	* Similar to Black Lion Trading Company, offers searchable and current market data.
